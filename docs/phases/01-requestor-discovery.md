# Phase 1: Who and What (Requestor Discovery)

**Status:** In progress

## Decisions so far

| Decision | Choice | Date |
|----------|--------|------|
| Primary user for v1 | The requestor: the patient's family or friend urgently needing blood | 2026-10-24 |
| Frontend | React (Vite) web app, wrapped for Android with Capacitor | 2026-10-24 |
| Project name | LifeDrop | 2026-10-24 |
| Home screen | "Request Blood" is the dominant action; donor and login are secondary | 2026-10-24 |
| Promise to keep | No account needed to request blood; request takes about a minute | 2026-10-24 |

Donors and hospitals/blood banks are secondary for now. They will be designed after the requestor flow is clear, but we keep them in mind so we don't paint ourselves into a corner.

## Open questions

To be answered next, one at a time:

1. Who exactly is the requestor, and what state are they in (stressed, on a phone, possibly in a hospital corridor with poor connectivity)?
2. What does the requestor need to know to make a request (blood group, units, hospital, deadline)?
3. What formalities do they face today, and which ones are truly necessary?
4. What counts as success for the requestor?
5. Which regions and languages come first?
