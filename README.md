# LifeDrop - Blood Donation App

An app that removes the paperwork and phone-tag from emergency blood requests, so people in a panic can get from "we need blood" to "a donor is on the way" as fast as possible.

> **Status:** Phase 1 – Requestor discovery (in progress)

## Why this exists

When a patient needs blood urgently, the family is stressed, time is short, and the process is full of formalities, forwarded messages, unverified numbers, and dead ends. This project aims to cut that friction.

## Guiding principles

- **Requestor first.** The first version is designed around the person who needs blood urgently.
- **Minimum steps in a crisis.** Every field, screen, and tap must justify its existence.
- **Trust matters.** Speed must not come at the cost of fake requests or donor safety.
- **Documented as we go.** Every phase records what we decided and why.
